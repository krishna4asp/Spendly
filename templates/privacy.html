Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. As you use Spendly, we store the expense records you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide.

We do not collect payment information, government IDs, or any sensitive financial credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Spendly service — displaying your expense history, generating summaries, and enabling you to manage your records. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

We will never sell your personal data or financial information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

You may delete your account and all associated data at any time. Upon deletion, your records are permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not currently integrate with or share data with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

If this changes in the future, this policy will be updated and you will be notified before any such integration takes effect.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact information provided in the application. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.
